About

Fifteen years on the mat. Eight coaching from it.

Amalia spent 15 years on Romania's national team in aerobic gymnastics — a gold medal at the European Championship (juniors) and a bronze at the World Championship (juniors) among the results. That's where the discipline comes from. Coaching, since 2018, is where she's spent it since.

Since 2019 she's built a wide practice across personal training, group fitness, and Pilates — currently teaching and training clients across several studios in Geneva, from reformer classes to private strength sessions. Alongside a master's degree in Nutrition and Quality of Life, she also works with clients on the fueling and recovery side of training, not just the training itself.
