About

Trained in the gym. Tempered on the mat.

Marlowe started coaching the way most strength coaches do — chasing bigger numbers on the bar. Years of client shoulders, hips, and lower backs later, she went and got certified in yoga and Pilates too, not to soften the strength work, but to make it last.

Now her sessions run on a simple loop: load something honestly, then teach the body to release it just as well. Clients range from first-time lifters to former athletes rebuilding range they lost to years of one-directional training.
